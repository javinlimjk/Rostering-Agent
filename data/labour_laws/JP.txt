Japan Labour Laws:

1. Labour Standards Act:
   - Standard working hours: 8 hours per day, 40 hours per week
   - Minimum rest period: 8 hours between shifts
   - Overtime requires 36 agreement

2. Rest Days:
   - At least one rest day per week
   - Maximum 6 consecutive working days

3. Overtime Limits:
   - Maximum 45 hours per month
   - Maximum 360 hours per year
   - Special circumstances allow up to 100 hours per month

4. Night Shifts:
   - Work between 10pm-5am requires 25% premium pay
   - Health considerations for prolonged night work
