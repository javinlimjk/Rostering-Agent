Singapore Labour Laws:

1. Working Hours:
   - Maximum 44 hours per week
   - Maximum 8 hours per day (or 9 hours if 5-day week)
   - Minimum rest period: 12 hours between shifts

2. Rest Days:
   - One rest day per week (continuous 30 hours)
   - Can work up to 7 consecutive days before rest day

3. Overtime:
   - Overtime pay required for hours exceeding 44 per week
   - Maximum 72 hours of overtime per month

4. Night Shifts:
   - Work between 10pm-6am considered night work
   - Additional allowances may apply
