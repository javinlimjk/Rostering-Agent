United States Labour Laws:

1. Working Hours:
   - Maximum 40 hours per week for standard employment
   - Overtime pay required for hours exceeding 40 per week
   - Minimum rest period: 11 hours between shifts

2. Rest Days:
   - Maximum 6 consecutive working days
   - At least one rest day per week

3. Night Shifts:
   - No specific restrictions but additional compensation may be required
   - Health and safety considerations must be met

4. Break Requirements:
   - Meal breaks required for shifts over 6 hours
   - Short rest breaks recommended every 4 hours
