United Kingdom Labour Laws:

1. Working Time Directive:
   - Maximum 48 hours per week (averaged over 17 weeks)
   - Workers can opt out voluntarily
   - Minimum rest period: 11 consecutive hours per 24-hour period

2. Rest Days:
   - Minimum one day off per week (or two days per fortnight)
   - Maximum 6 consecutive working days

3. Night Work:
   - Night workers should not work more than 8 hours in any 24-hour period
   - Night time defined as 11pm to 6am

4. Break Requirements:
   - 20-minute rest break if working more than 6 hours
