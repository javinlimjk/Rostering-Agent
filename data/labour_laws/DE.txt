Germany Labour Laws:

1. Working Time Act (Arbeitszeitgesetz):
   - Maximum 8 hours per day (can extend to 10 hours)
   - Maximum 48 hours per week
   - Minimum rest period: 11 consecutive hours between shifts

2. Rest Days:
   - Sundays and public holidays are generally rest days
   - Maximum 6 consecutive working days

3. Night Work:
   - Night work defined as 11pm to 6am
   - Night workers maximum 8 hours per 24-hour period
   - Health checks required for regular night workers

4. Break Requirements:
   - 30-minute break for 6-9 hours work
   - 45-minute break for over 9 hours work
